# Contributing


- **Have an idea, a question or a complaint?** [Open an issue](../../issues/new)
- **Want to change something?** Open a pull request

## Constraints

British English, single quotes for dialogue, I've left out the full series outline in the repo so nobody gets spoiled. Plot ideas welcome, that's where we can really get our hands dirty.

By contributing, you agree to share your work under the [LICENSE](LICENSE): CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for story text, MIT for code.
